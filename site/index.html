Inspect the source ↗
What does Harmoness actually check?

Duration, sample peaks, quiet passages below 0.002 RMS for at least a second, level shifts of at least 8 dB between adjacent five-second windows, and a heuristic for abrupt endings. Every flag has a listening location. These checks do not detect every artifact or judge musical quality.

How fair is the comparison?

Level matching uses whole-track RMS and attenuation only, with sample-peak headroom. It is not perceptual LUFS matching. Hide names or shuffle before listening; preferences remain your judgment. Shuffling is an informal comparison aid, not a controlled study.

Does the harmony check understand my song?

It estimates pitch-class energy in declared time regions. It cannot prove motif preservation, identify moral qualities, or certify artistic success. The browser result is a preview; the repository’s PCM-WAV verifier remains the canonical v0 witness.

Where does my audio go?

Nowhere off-device. Importing decodes and measures locally. Save on device stores audio in this browser using IndexedDB. Opening Compose also saves your current session to preserve the round trip. Reports contain measurements, notes, and prompts, not audio. There are no analytics or audio uploads.

Do I need Suno or a paid account?

No. Bring supported audio from any source. Harmoness does not call a generation service. The composition studio helps write directions you can use elsewhere. The demo is synthesized locally, not a Suno recording.

What’s with the cowgirl?

Harmony + harness = Harmoness. A little portamento, a little portmanteau. Our original illustrated studio sidekick brings the attitude. Your ears make the decisions.
